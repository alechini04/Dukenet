# Landing (home)

Scope: the single-page public landing of DukeNet. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: Colombian small/mid business owner, often on a phone, deciding whether DukeNet is serious enough to talk to. Action: open WhatsApp or send the quote form (which composes a WhatsApp message). Proof on hand: El Gato Galletero and Suè (live), Convite (in construction); dashboard numbers are illustrative and labeled. Constraints: no prices, testimonials or invented metrics; stock photos are placeholders to replace.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every DukeNet site is a construction project with a responsible builder, a delivery date and visible progress. The page is a municipal "valla de obra" and its site paperwork, refusing the dark-agency hero with giant condensed type and moody photos.

OWN-WORLD: User-pinned palette, translated: steel-blue informative road-sign panels (#5980a6 with its 100–900 ramp) drenching whole regions with white sign lettering, black (#0c0d0e) night-site ground and sign ink, reflective off-white (#f2f2f3) plates, black/white barrier stripes used only as section thresholds, concrete greys for secondary. No yellow or orange. Highway-sign lettering (Overpass heavy) for display, Overpass Mono for plate codes. Square corners, bolted plates, ficha técnica tables, frente codes FR-01…FR-07.

STORY: The visitor reads "No hacemos páginas bonitas, hacemos ventas" as a site license, sees what gets built (four obra types A–D), finished works, the five-phase schedule, the data they will get, and requests their own obra over WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed steel-blue valla. Top: black strip with DUKENET wordmark, frente nav, WhatsApp plate. Left two-thirds: headline in three lines at 11–13vw, black. Right/below: ficha técnica table (Proyecto, Constructor, Cobertura, Entrega, Avance with animated bar). Primary action "Cotizar mi obra" black plate bottom-left plus WhatsApp.

FORM: Valla de obra, position 6 on the ordered list, seed key 1c17d731.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: A single avance band travels the page with scroll (progress + current frente code), and the Cronograma section is "obra en vivo": a pinned stage where scroll builds the El Gato Galletero home from diagnosis notes to wireframe, design skeleton, live screenshot and monthly report (added by user request for overdrive, 2026-09-16); service variants A–D render solid when selected, dashed otherwise; dashboard numbers move with damped ballistics and show trend.

RECORDED ADAPTATIONS (after finish review round 1): The headline runs at min(8.8vw, 15.5svh), max 9.4rem, instead of 11–13vw so the full valla (headline, pitch, ficha) holds in one 1440x900 viewport. The white/black split is kept because the ink line carries the "hacemos ventas" claim and the struck white line reads as the refused promise. The "Avance" bar became an ETAPAS stepper cycling through the five real phases, because a percentage would be an invented progress number. The header top strip carries COTIZAR; WhatsApp stays one tap away through the hero plate and the floating ESCRÍBENOS plate. Sign panels keep a road-sign radius; every other surface is square.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
