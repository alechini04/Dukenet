# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Astro (static output) + GSAP with ScrollTrigger/SplitText + Lenis smooth scroll. The user asked for "whatever looks best with top-tier animation"; Astro ships near-zero JS by default so the motion budget goes to GSAP, and the output deploys free as static files (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel).

## Users

Owners of small and mid-size businesses across Colombia (shops, fashion brands, restaurants, service providers, founders launching a product) who sell mostly through WhatsApp, Instagram or in person and are deciding whether to invest in a website. They usually arrive from a referral, an ad or a WhatsApp link, often on a phone, and want to know: is this studio serious, what would I get, how much hassle, and how do I talk to someone.

## Product Purpose

DukeNet is a web development studio. It designs and hand-codes custom websites (online stores, business pages, campaign landings) from scratch, installs analytics, and accompanies the client month to month with a report and adjustments. Success for the landing: a qualified business owner starts a WhatsApp conversation or sends the quote form.

## Positioning

No templates: every site is designed and programmed from zero around how that business sells, then measured and improved monthly. The pitch is "we don't make pretty pages, we make sales" — a sales channel with data, not a brochure.

## Operating Context

- Contact is WhatsApp-first: +57 316 328 9924 and +57 320 394 5482. The quote form composes a WhatsApp message (name, business and city, what they need, details) and opens wa.me.
- Service lines: Tienda en línea (payments, shipping, inventory, coupons, combos, training), Página de negocio (services, menu, portfolio, WhatsApp contact), Landing de campaña (launches, promos, paid traffic), Datos y acompañamiento (Spanish dashboard, monthly report, improvements).
- Method: Diagnóstico (free, written) → Propuesta (fixed price and date) → Diseño (see it before it exists) → Lanzamiento (hand-coded, mobile tested, payments/shipping/analytics set up) → Crecer (monthly report and adjustments).
- Serves all of Colombia (Bucaramanga, Bogotá, Medellín, Cali, Barranquilla, Cúcuta, Pereira, Santa Marta named in copy). Takes few projects per month.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Spanish (Colombia) only.
- Static site, no backend; forms hand off to WhatsApp.
- Prices are not published; undecided.
- No custom photography supplied yet: use neutral stock imagery as placeholders and list them for replacement.

## Brand Commitments

- Name written DUKENET with "NET" set apart (the v4 design colors it in the accent).
- Voice: direct, confident, plain Spanish, tuteo, no jargon, anti-template.
- Palette pinned by the user (2026-09-16): the steel blue from the original design (#5980a6 and its tonal ramp), greys, black (#0c0d0e) and white (#f2f2f3). Other hues may be added only if they clearly serve the page.

## Evidence on Hand

- Case studies (real, in production): El Gato Galletero — https://www.elgatogalletero.com/ (store with a cause, supports cat rescue); Suè — https://suue.shop/ (handmade knitwear brand, collection launch). Convite — wedding platform (digital invitation, RSVP, gift list), still in construction.
- Dashboard figures in the design (4.812 visits, 167 orders, 3,4% conversion, $78.400 ticket) are illustrative and must stay labeled as example.
- No testimonials, client logos, awards, prices or metrics exist. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Sell outcomes, not decoration: every section moves the visitor toward a conversation.
2. Show that it is custom: the site itself must be the proof that DukeNet does not use templates.
3. Plain talk and transparency: fixed price, date, and what you receive, before you sign.
4. The relationship continues after launch: data and monthly accompaniment are part of the offer.
5. WhatsApp is the front door; it must be one tap away everywhere.
